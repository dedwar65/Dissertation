I wanted to ask whether you would be willing to serve as an external member of my PhD thesis committee. My defense is currently scheduled for April 2026, and the other committee members are Christopher Carroll, Nick Papageorge, and Stelios Fourakis, all from the JHU Department of Economics.

My dissertation, *Essays on Heterogeneous Returns, Trust, and Household Wealth*, studies heterogeneity in asset returns and its implications for wealth accumulation and inequality through three essays. My job market paper extends the standard heterogeneous-agent consumption-saving framework to allow for differences in rates of return across households and calibrates the resulting model to match the empirical wealth distribution in the Survey of Consumer Finances. I also compare the distributional and welfare effects of revenue-equivalent wealth and capital income tax policies in this setting. My second essay uses the RAND Longitudinal version of the Health and Retirement Study to document a hump-shaped relationship between generalized trust and returns to net wealth, while characterizing the persistent, individual-level component of returns using panel regression models. My third essay develops a decision-theoretic framework for ranking wealth distributions under ambiguity and applies the social welfare approach to derive a measure of wealth inequality aversion, which I compare to the Black-White median wealth gap using triennial SCF data from 1989–2022.

My job market paper involves calibrating a heterogeneous-agent model by solving for the distribution of asset returns that best matches empirical wealth moments — a structured optimization problem over a discrete (or lognormal) type space, subject to equilibrium constraints from households' dynamic savings problems. Your expertise in the design and analysis of algorithms for problems combining discrete choices and continuous optimization is directly relevant to the computational structure of this work.

I would be happy to share a draft of my dissertation. Please let me know if you have any questions or would like any additional information.
